Hi, I’m Owain Buchnea! As a student at the University of Guelph majoring in Computer Science and minoring in Project Management, I’ve always had an interest in all things technical from architecture and engineering to astrophysics. Additionally, I absolutely adore all forms of art, especially experimental visual design and music.

Outside academics I involve myself in cardistry, an artform consisting of flourishes and movement sculptures of playing cards in all sorts of manners. In 2024 I started MOMENTOUS, a cardistry brand centered around the idea that many of us feel stuck in this late-stage capitalist hellscape, and to bring momentous change to your life to flip things around for hope of a better future, both for yourself and for society at large.

Feel free to check out the of the site — I have a gallery of all my personal graphic design work, co-op work-term overviews, and more!
